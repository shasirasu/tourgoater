# Puducherry listing research

The Puducherry catalog was expanded from four to ten visitor experiences on 28 July 2026. Descriptions intentionally avoid promising opening hours, boat departures, swimming safety, or entry because those conditions can change.

## Information sources

- Government of Puducherry tourism overview: https://www.py.gov.in/tourism-0
- Puducherry Tourism Department visitor FAQ: https://pondytourism.py.gov.in/onlinebs/FAQ.aspx
- Ministry of Tourism destination master plan for Puducherry, including Pondy Marina food courts and its proximity to White Town: https://sd2.tourism.gov.in/DocumentRepoFiles/MasterPlan/MP425125b3-8141-4109-bdf1-56b07aa5d227.pdf
- Official Auroville visitor information: https://auroville.org/
